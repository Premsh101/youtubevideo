🧸 Sunave Kids: setup guide

Create the YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account, connect them to the studio, and publish from one button.

0 · Before you start 10 min

Your app address in these steps is https://YOUR-DOMAIN. (When you open this page from the studio, the real address is filled in for you.)

What you need

  • A Google account that will own the channel. A dedicated one, e.g. sunavekids@gmail.com, is best.
  • A personal Facebook account. Meta requires a person to own a Page, but the Page will show only "Sunave Kids".
  • A phone number for YouTube verification and the Meta developer account.
  • A logo, square, at least 800 × 800 px, plus a YouTube banner (2560 × 1440) and a Facebook cover (1640 × 624). Canva has free kids-channel templates.

One channel or one per language? YouTube recommends videos better when a channel sticks to one language, so the big kids' brands run a channel per language. The studio supports both: connect a Hindi channel and Hindi videos go there automatically, while everything else goes to the main channel. If you'd rather start simple, use one channel and add the Hindi channel later.

1 · Create the YouTube channel 20 min

1.1 Create the channel (as a Brand channel)

  1. Go to youtube.com and sign in with the Google account from step 0.
  2. Click your avatar (top right), then Settings, then Add or manage your channel(s), then Create a channel.
  3. Name: Sunave Kids. Tick the agreement and click Create.
    A Brand channel can have managers and lets one Google account own several channels (main + Hindi).

1.2 Mark the channel "Made for kids" (required)

  1. Open studio.youtube.com, then Settings (bottom left), then Channel, then Advanced settings.
  2. Under Audience, choose Yes, set this channel as made for kids, then Save.
This is mandatory for toddler content under COPPA and FTC rules, and getting it wrong can lead to fines. Comments, the notification bell, mini-player and personalised ads are switched off on kids' videos; that's normal for every kids' channel. The studio also marks every upload "made for kids".

1.3 Profile, handle and branding

  1. In Studio: Customization, then Profile (called Basic info on some accounts).
    • Handle: @SunaveKids
    • Description (paste and edit): 
      Welcome to Sunave Kids! 🌈 Happy nursery rhymes and baby songs in Hindi and English for toddlers aged 1–3. Sing along with our friendly animal friends, and learn colours, numbers, animals and good habits.

New rhymes every week. Subscribe and sing along! 🎵

हिंदी बालगीत और नर्सरी राइम्स, छोटे बच्चों के लिए।
    • Links: add your Instagram and Facebook Page once they exist (step 3).
    • Contact email for brands and partnerships.
  2. Customization, then Branding: profile picture (logo, 800 × 800), banner (2560 × 1440, keep text inside the centre 1546 × 423 area), and a video watermark (150 × 150 "Subscribe" bubble). Click Publish.

1.4 Verify the channel

  1. Open youtube.com/verify and verify by SMS. This unlocks custom thumbnails, which the studio uploads automatically, and videos over 15 minutes.

1.5 Defaults, country and keywords

  1. Studio: Settings, then Channel, then Basic info. Country: India. Keywords: 
    Sunave Kids, nursery rhymes, kids songs, baby songs, toddler songs, hindi rhymes, balgeet, hindi nursery rhymes, bachon ke gaane, learning songs
  2. Settings, then Upload defaults: category Education, video language Hindi or English to match the channel. Click Save.

1.6 Playlists (manual, once)

Studio: Content, then Playlists, then New playlist. Start with: Hindi Rhymes | हिंदी बालगीतEnglish Nursery RhymesBedtime LullabiesLearning Songs (colours, numbers, animals). Add new videos to them after they're published. Playlists greatly increase watch time.

1.7 Hindi channel (optional)

Repeat 1.1 to 1.5 with the name Sunave Kids Hindi and handle @SunaveKidsHindi.

2 · Connect YouTube to the studio 15 min

Do this in the same Google Cloud project you use for Gemini/Vertex. If you already did part C of DEPLOY.md, check 2.2 especially.

2.1 Enable the API

  1. Google Cloud console: YouTube Data API v3, then Enable.

2.2 Consent screen: set to "In production"

  1. Console: APIs & Services, then OAuth consent screen (on new consoles this is Google Auth Platform).
  2. Branding: app name Sunave Kids Studio, support email = yours. Audience: External.
  3. Under Audience, click Publish app, then Confirm, so the status shows In production.
If the app stays in Testing, Google expires the login after 7 days and uploads start failing weekly. "In production" without verification is fine for your own use. You'll see a "Google hasn't verified this app" screen when connecting, which is expected.

2.3 OAuth client

  1. APIs & Services, then Credentials, then + Create credentials, then OAuth client ID.
  2. Application type Web application, name studio.
  3. Authorised redirect URIs, then + Add URI: 
    https://YOUR-DOMAIN/youtube/oauth2callback
  4. Create, then Download JSON.

2.4 Give the JSON to the studio

  1. Coolify: your resource, then Storages, then + Add, then File mount. Destination /srv/data/secrets/youtube_client_secret.json, content = the downloaded JSON. Click Save, then Redeploy.

2.5 Connect the channel(s)

  1. Open the studio and click 📡 Publishing accounts (top right), then ➕ Connect main channel.
  2. Pick the Google account. On the channel chooser pick Sunave Kids, not your personal channel.
  3. "Google hasn't verified this app": click Advanced, then Go to Sunave Kids Studio. Tick both permissions and click Continue.
  4. You're returned to the studio and the panel shows ▶ Sunave Kids, all languages.
  5. Hindi channel: in the same panel choose Hindi in the dropdown, click ➕ Connect a channel for this language, and pick Sunave Kids Hindi.
Daily limit: YouTube gives every project 10,000 API units per day. One upload uses about 1,600 units, plus 400 for captions and 50 for the thumbnail, so you get roughly 5 uploads a day (Shorts count as uploads). That's plenty for a steady schedule. For more, apply via YouTube's API quota extension form.

3 · Facebook Page & Instagram 20 min

3.1 Facebook Page

  1. On facebook.com/pages/create, logged in with your personal account:
    • Page name: Sunave Kids
    • Category: type and pick Video Creator and Education
    • Bio: "Happy nursery rhymes & baby songs in Hindi and English for little ones 🌈 New rhymes every week!"
    Then click Create Page.
  2. Add the profile picture (logo) and cover photo (1640 × 624), then Next. Skip WhatsApp and inviting friends, then Done.
  3. Optional: Page Settings, then Username: SunaveKids.

3.2 Instagram: professional account

  1. In the Instagram app, create the account @sunavekids (Profile, then your username at the top, then Add account, then Create new account).
  2. Profile, then ☰, then Settings and activity, then Account type and tools, then Switch to professional account.
  3. Category: Video Creator (or Education). Choose Business, then Done.
    Automatic Reel posting through the API works with Professional accounts; Business is the safest choice.
  4. Edit profile: name Sunave Kids, the logo, the bio, and add your YouTube channel link.

3.3 Link Instagram to the Facebook Page (required for automation)

  1. On Facebook, switch to the Page: avatar, then See all profiles, then Sunave Kids.
  2. Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Linked accounts, then Instagram, then Connect account.
  3. Log in as @sunavekids and allow access.

Alternative: Meta Business Suite, then Settings, then Business assets, then Instagram accounts, then Connect.

3.4 Business portfolio (recommended)

  1. business.facebook.com: Create a business portfolio named Sunave Kids. Then Settings, then Accounts, then Pages and Instagram accounts, and add both. This makes the developer app setup in step 4 smoother and lets you add helpers later without sharing passwords.
Instagram and Facebook require users to be 13 or older, so write posts for parents ("Sing along with your little one!"). The studio's Facebook and Instagram text already does this.

4 · Meta developer app 20 min

This is what lets the studio post for you. It stays in Development mode: you're its admin and post only to your own Page and Instagram, so no App Review is needed.

4.1 Developer account

  1. developers.facebook.com, then Get started (log in with the Facebook account that owns the Page). Verify your phone and accept the terms.

4.2 Create the app

  1. My Apps, then Create app.
  2. App name Sunave Kids Studio, contact email = yours, then Next.
  3. Use case: Other, then Next. App type Business, then Next.
  4. Business portfolio: Sunave Kids (from 3.4), then Create app and confirm your password.
Meta redesigns this wizard often. If you only see use cases, pick "Manage everything on your Page" and add "Manage messaging & content on Instagram" (choose API setup with Facebook login). The end result is the same.

4.3 Add the products

  1. App dashboard: Add product, then Facebook Login for Business, then Set up.
  2. Its Settings: Client OAuth login = Yes, Web OAuth login = Yes, Enforce HTTPS = Yes. Valid OAuth Redirect URIs: 
    https://YOUR-DOMAIN/meta/oauth2callback
    Then Save changes.
  3. Add product, then Instagram, then Set up, and choose API setup with Facebook login if asked.

4.4 Login configuration (permissions)

  1. Facebook Login for Business, then Configurations, then + Create configuration.
  2. Name Publishing. Login variation General. Access token User access token.
  3. Assets: Pages and Instagram accounts. Permissions, tick exactly:
    pages_show_listpages_read_engagementpages_manage_postsbusiness_managementinstagram_basicinstagram_content_publish
  4. Create, then copy the Configuration ID. This is META_LOGIN_CONFIG_ID.

If your app shows classic Facebook Login instead (no Configurations menu), skip 4.4 and leave META_LOGIN_CONFIG_ID empty. The studio then asks for those permissions directly.

4.5 App ID and secret

  1. App settings, then Basic. Copy the App ID and, after Show, the App secret.
  2. Privacy policy URL: Meta asks for one. Any page with your privacy text works, e.g. a Google Doc shared publicly. Click Save changes.
  3. Leave the top toggle at Development. Don't switch to Live.
Keep the App secret private, like a password. Put it only in Coolify (step 5), never in chats or screenshots.

5 · Coolify settings 5 min

  1. Coolify: your resource, then Environment Variables, then Developer view. Add or adjust: 
    CHANNEL_NAME=Sunave Kids
PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://YOUR-DOMAIN
YOUTUBE_REDIRECT_URI=https://YOUR-DOMAIN/youtube/oauth2callback
META_APP_ID=paste-app-id
META_APP_SECRET=paste-app-secret
META_LOGIN_CONFIG_ID=paste-configuration-id
YOUTUBE_PRIVACY=private
REEL_MAX_SECONDS=90
SHORT_MAX_SECONDS=175
  2. Save, then Redeploy. Wait for Running.

PUBLIC_BASE_URL matters because Facebook and Instagram download the video from your server. The studio gives them a signed link that expires after 3 days, so nothing else is exposed.

6 · Connect Facebook & Instagram 3 min

  1. Studio: 📡 Publishing accounts, then ➕ Connect Facebook & Instagram.
  2. Meta dialog: Continue as …. When asked which business, pick Sunave Kids.
  3. Pages: tick Sunave Kids. Instagram accounts: tick @sunavekids. Then Continue and Save.
  4. Back in the studio the panel shows 📘 Page: Sunave Kids and 📸 Instagram: @sunavekids, and the header badges turn ✓.
The login doesn't expire: the studio swaps it for a long-lived Page token. If you change your Facebook password or remove the app, click 🔄 Reconnect.

6½ · Logo watermark on every video 2 min

  1. Studio: 📡 Publishing accounts, then scroll to 🎨 Logo watermark, then ⬆ Upload logo, and pick your Sunave Kids logo (PNG, JPG or WebP; a white background is fine).
  2. The studio removes the white background, including the insides of letters like a, e and d, keeps white outlines such as the one around "Kids", trims the edges and adds a soft shadow. Check the preview on the checkered background.
  3. When asked "Apply it to all existing videos now too?", click OK. Every finished video is re-rendered with the logo. This costs nothing in AI usage and takes about 1–2 minutes per video.

The logo sits at the very bottom-right, about 17% of the video width and slightly transparent, so it brands the video without distracting little ones. On-screen lyrics move up just above it. Shorts and Reels inherit it. Videos already posted to YouTube, Facebook or Instagram keep their old version, so publish them again if you want the logo there too.

To change the position or size, set LOGO_POSITION (right, left or center) and LOGO_WIDTH_PCT (e.g. 0.15) in Coolify, redeploy, then click ✨ Apply logo to all existing videos.

7 · Publishing a video 1 min per video

  1. Create a video as usual and watch both language versions in the result card.
  2. Under each language there's a 🚀 Publish box. Tick the platforms:
    OptionWhat gets posted
    ▶ YouTube videoThe full 16:9 video with title, description, tags, hashtags, thumbnail and lyrics captions, marked made for kids. Goes to that language's channel if connected.
    📱 YouTube ShortA 9:16 version (blurred background, nothing cropped), up to about 3 minutes, linking to the full video.
    📘 Facebook PageThe full video posted on the Sunave Kids Page, with the YouTube link.
    📸 Instagram ReelA 9:16 Reel of up to 90 seconds, cut right after a complete scene (never mid-line), with caption, hashtags and cover.
  3. Choose the YouTube privacy, and optionally a date and time to schedule YouTube to go public.
  4. Click 🚀 Publish selected. Each row shows ⏳ uploading, then ✓ posted (click to open), or ✗ with the reason. Instagram takes 1–3 minutes to process.
Facebook and Instagram posts are public immediately. A safe routine: publish YouTube as private, check it in YouTube Studio, then publish again to Facebook and Instagram (already-posted rows stay ✓ and unticked).

8 · Growing Sunave Kids

  • Consistency beats volume. Aim for 3 full videos a week per channel, plus Shorts and Reels cut from them.
  • Posting times (IST) when parents are on their phones: 7–9 AM, 12–2 PM and 7–9 PM. Weekends work well. Use the schedule box.
  • Series and playlists: recurring characters (the studio reuses them automatically) and themed playlists keep toddlers watching back to back.
  • End screens (manual): YouTube Studio, then the video, then Editor, then End screen. Add Subscribe and Best for viewer.
  • Thumbnails: one big friendly character face and bright colours. The studio's auto thumbnail is a starting point; a custom one often doubles clicks.
  • Policies that protect reach:
    • Never use other channels' names (Cocomelon, ChuChu…) in titles or tags. The studio already avoids this.
    • YouTube's inauthentic content rule demonetises mass-produced, repetitive uploads. Vary rhymes, topics and characters, and review every video before it goes public.
    • Cartoon animation doesn't need YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" label; the studio sets it to No.
  • Monetisation (YouTube Partner Program): 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers plus 10 million Shorts views in 90 days. Made-for-kids videos earn lower ad rates, so volume and watch time matter.

9 · Troubleshooting

You seeFix
"YouTube OAuth client JSON missing"Step 2.4: the file mount path must be exactly /srv/data/secrets/youtube_client_secret.json. Redeploy.
Google error redirect_uri_mismatchStep 2.3: the URI must match YOUTUBE_REDIRECT_URI exactly, including https and no trailing slash.
YouTube uploads fail after a weekStep 2.2: the consent screen is still in Testing. Publish it, then reconnect.
quotaExceeded from YouTubeDaily 10,000-unit limit reached (about 5 uploads). It resets at midnight Pacific time; try again later.
Meta "URL blocked" or "Can't load URL"Step 4.3: redirect URI /meta/oauth2callback missing, or PUBLIC_BASE_URL differs from your domain.
"No Facebook Pages were shared with the app"In the Meta dialog tick the Page. Click 🔄 Reconnect and choose Edit access.
Instagram "not linked to this Page"Step 3.2 (must be Professional) and step 3.3 (linked to the Page), then 🔄 Reconnect.
Instagram ✗ "could not process the video"Usually length: lower REEL_MAX_SECONDS (e.g. 60) in Coolify and publish again.
Facebook or Instagram can't download the videoPUBLIC_BASE_URL must be the public https address and the app must be reachable from the internet.
